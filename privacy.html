1. Introduction

Wyoming Insulation ("we," "us," or "our") operates Wyoming Insulation Work Orders ("App"). This policy explains how information is accessed, used, and handled when you use the App, including when you authorize a QuickBooks Online connection.

2. Information We May Access

With your authorization, the App may access information needed to generate work orders, such as customer names and business contact information; job addresses and project details; estimate numbers and dates; estimate descriptions and line items; product or service descriptions, quantities, and measurements; and related estimate information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through QuickBooks is intended to retrieve authorized estimates, populate customer and job details, convert estimate line items into work-order tasks, generate printable work orders, and maintain the App. QuickBooks customer or business information is not used to sell advertising or build unrelated marketing profiles.

4. Current Development Status and Storage

The current prototype is designed to operate locally in the user's browser and does not currently provide a hosted customer database. The QuickBooks integration is under development. Before introducing hosted storage or additional data-processing services, we will update this policy to describe the actual services, storage locations, and processing practices. Users should keep downloaded work orders secure, especially if they contain customer contact information or job addresses.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information or QuickBooks business data. Information may be shared as reasonably necessary with Intuit and QuickBooks Online to establish an authorized connection; with service providers required to operate the App if introduced; when required by law or valid legal process; or to protect users or Wyoming Insulation.

6. Third-Party Services

The App is intended to use Intuit services to connect to QuickBooks Online. Your use of QuickBooks is also governed by Intuit's terms and privacy policies. We do not control Intuit's privacy practices or service availability. The App is independently operated by Wyoming Insulation and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Intuit Inc.

7. Security

We intend to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information handled by the App. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. The App is intended to use Intuit's authorization process rather than asking users to provide their QuickBooks passwords directly to the App.

8. Retention and Deletion

The current local prototype does not operate a central hosted database of customer or estimate information. Users control files they download or save on their devices. Delete files when no longer needed, subject to business recordkeeping requirements. If future versions store information on our systems, this policy will be updated to explain retention and deletion procedures.

9. Your Choices

You may revoke the App's QuickBooks access through the appropriate Intuit account or connected-app settings. You may contact us with questions or requests about information handled by the App. Additional rights may apply depending on your location and applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the App develops or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be posted at the public URL where this policy is made available.
